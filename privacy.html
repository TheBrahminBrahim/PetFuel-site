Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PetFuel ("we", "our", or "the app") is a nutrition tracker for dogs and cats on iOS, made by Scarabyte. It's built to work on your device first — you don't need an account to use it. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Without an account, your pets and meal logs stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Apple, your data is backed up to our servers so it syncs and can be shared with family.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no location, no advertising identifiers.
  • We never sell your data.

What data the app handles

  • Pet profiles — your pet's name, species, breed, sex, birthday, weight and body-condition history, diet preferences, allergies, and optionally a profile photo.
  • Meal logs — ingredients and amounts you enter, commercial foods you scan or search for, and the nutrition analysis the app computes.
  • Account information (only if you sign in) — Sign in with Apple gives us an account ID and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), and your first name on first sign-in. We use your first name as a display name you can change.
  • Family sharing (only if you use it) — which family you belong to, your role, and invite codes. Your display name is shown to family members next to the meals you log.
  • Subscription status — whether you have PetFuel Pro, verified through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • App settings and reminders — preferences such as units and theme, and meal reminders, which are scheduled on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • We don't collect your location.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising tools.
  • We don't see or store your payment details — Apple handles all payments.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.

Where your data is stored

On your device. By default, everything is stored locally on your iPhone. Photos you take of meals and your pet's profile photo stay on your device.

On our servers, if you sign in. When you sign in with Apple, your pet profiles, meal logs, weight and body-condition entries, and family membership are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, so they can sync across devices and be shared with family members you invite. Data is encrypted in transit. Our servers may be located outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws.

Camera and photos

PetFuel uses your camera to scan barcodes and product labels and to photograph a meal, and your photo library so you can choose a profile photo for your pet. We only access them when you use those features.

When you use the AI photo feature ("Snap") or scan a nutrition label, that photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to estimate the meal or read the label. Google processes it under its own terms; we don't keep a copy on our servers. Avoid including people or personal information in these photos.

Services we share data with

We share data only with the services that make the app work:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases.
  • Supabase — hosts account, sync, and family-sharing data if you sign in.
  • RevenueCat — confirms your subscription status using an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase records. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Google (Gemini) — processes photos you submit to the AI photo and label-scan features.
  • USDA FoodData Central — receives the ingredient searches you type, to look up nutrition values.
  • Open Pet Food Facts — receives barcodes and product searches to look up commercial foods.
  • Family members you invite — see the pets you share with them, their logs, and your display name.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Contributing to Open Pet Food Facts (optional)

There's an optional setting, off by default, to help build the public pet-food database. If you turn it on, details from labels you confirm — barcode, product name, brand, size, ingredients, calories, guaranteed analysis, and any "complete and balanced" claim — are sent to Open Pet Food Facts and published openly. We don't send your name, account, or pet information with it.

Nutritional data and accuracy

For home-cooked and ingredient-based meals, nutritional values are computed from the USDA FoodData Central database. For commercially-produced pet foods, we display only what's published: calories, macros, and whether the product is labeled "complete and balanced."

We never fabricate or estimate micronutrient data for commercial foods. Pet-food labels don't publish those numbers, so we don't invent them — if we don't know, we say so.

PetFuel provides nutritional information only — it is not a substitute for veterinary advice. Consult your veterinarian for any health or medical concerns.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device — Settings → "Delete all data" erases your pets and logs from your phone. Deleting the app also removes them.
  • On our servers — to delete your account and the data stored with it, email privacy@petfuel.app from the email linked to your Apple ID (or tell us your account's relay address). We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Family data — if you leave or delete a family, meals you logged for pets owned by someone else may remain in their records.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel a PetFuel Pro subscription — cancel it in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's privacy laws, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent. Email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local privacy authority.

Children

PetFuel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
