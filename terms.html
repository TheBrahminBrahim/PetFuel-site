2. Nutrition data and estimates

  • Homemade meals — nutrient values are calculated from the USDA FoodData Central database and the amounts you enter. Real ingredients vary, so results are estimates.
  • Commercial foods — we show only what's available from the label or product databases: calories, macros, and whether the food states it is "complete and balanced." We don't invent micronutrient values we don't have.
  • AI photo and label features — some features send a photo you take to an AI service (currently Google Gemini) to estimate a bowl's contents or read a label. These estimates can be wrong; review and edit them before saving.
  • Targets — daily targets are reference values based on the details you enter (like weight and life stage). They are not personalised medical recommendations, and they're only as accurate as the information you provide.
